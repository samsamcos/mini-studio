The Pipeline

14 stages, one button

Every stage runs automatically. A failure in any stage is logged as a warning and the pipeline carries on — you always get something useful at the end.

01 🔍 Probe

ffprobe reads duration, resolution, bitrate. 4K sources handled correctly throughout.

02 🎙️ Transcribe

faster-whisper with word-level timestamps. Every word timed for karaoke captions.

03 ✂️ Dead Air

Silence detection removes gaps. On a 20-min vlog this is often 3–5 minutes cut.

04 🚩 Bad Takes

Five patterns: stutters, filler overload, restarts, fragments, repeated phrases.

05 📊 Loudness

ebur128 loudness curve every 0.1s — the energy map the scorer uses.

06 ⭐ Highlights

Sliding windows scored on loudness, speech density, and keywords. Best 2% kept.

07 ✍️ Script Polish

Gemma 2 9B cleans the speech. Strict rules prevent it inventing anything not in the source.

08 🗣️ TTS

Pocket TTS speaks it in the character's voice. 3.3× realtime, CPU-only.

09 🔊 Audio Clean

Highpass → neural noise suppression → loudnorm I=−16 LUFS (broadcast standard).

10 ⏱️ Timing Fit

If narration overruns the footage, the last clip extends to cover it. No black frames.

11 💬 Captions

SRT + styled ASS captions with per-word timing for karaoke animation in OpenCut.

12 📑 Chapters

Speech gaps become chapter boundaries. YouTube-format markers, max 12.

13 🖼️ Thumbnails

6 most visually distinct frames extracted. Pick the best one.

14 🎞️ Timeline

OpenCut timeline.json + 9:16 vertical. Clips point at the original — trim handles work.

Features

Built for the whole workflow

Every piece connects. Nothing is a one-off script.

🎭

Character Profiles

JARVIS, ARIA, Narrator — or build your own. Each character carries a voice, narration tone, caption style, zoom frequency, and vertical crop mode.

📺

Channel Profiles

Per-channel editing dials — silence threshold, cut speed, caption font, TTS pace. Set once, applied to every job for that channel.

🔄

Non-destructive Edits

Timeline clips reference the original file with trimStart/trimEnd. Drag any cut longer in OpenCut without re-encoding or re-running the pipeline.

🔁

Clip Swap

Every highlight has pre-computed alternates. Don't like clip 3? Swap it for the next-ranked option with one click — no re-analysis.

📱

9:16 Vertical

Centre-crop or blur-background modes. Generates the Shorts/Reels version automatically alongside the widescreen edit.

🧠

On-Demand AI

LLMs start on first request, stop after 10 min idle. Idle RAM: ~0.8 GB. Both models loaded: ~7.3 GB. Your machine stays usable.

🛡️

Fault Tolerant

Every stage is wrapped in a soft() handler. A TTS timeout or Gemini quota hit logs a warning and the pipeline keeps going. You always get output.

⚡

CPU-Only

No GPU required. Runs on a standard VM or mini-PC. Whisper small, Gemma 2 9B Q4, and Pocket TTS all run comfortably on CPU.

🎯

Source-Grounded Script

Gemma's prompt enforces strict rules: temperature 0.1, no invented facts, word count derived from available footage at 2.3 wps.
